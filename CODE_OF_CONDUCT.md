# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be direct, be kind, be useful. This project exists to help creatives articulate their business. Contributions and discussions should reflect that intent.

## Expected Behavior

- Give constructive feedback with specific suggestions
- Respect that coaching is personal — people's business identities are tied to their self-image
- Disagree on approach, not on people
- Accept that the maintainer has final say on voice, tone, and coaching philosophy

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Spamming issues or PRs with promotional content
- Any conduct that would make someone uncomfortable contributing

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior can be reported to support@unknowncreatives.studio. The maintainer will review and respond as appropriate, up to and including banning from the project.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any related communication channels.

# Eval: Observation Map Integrity

## Applies To
Analyze-conversations skill — the observation map produced after reading materials

## Criteria
- Only themes with actual signal are included — no padding, no manufactured observations to fill sections
- Confidence is calibrated to the volume of data: 2-3 conversations yield hypotheses, 10+ yield patterns
- With a single conversation, the coach is transparent that it's reading a snapshot, not a pattern
- Where knowledge files exist (positioning.md, ideal-client-profile.md), observations are cross-referenced against them

## Pass Indicators
- Sections with no signal are omitted from the observation map entirely
- Confidence language matches data volume ("This showed up in one conversation, so I'm holding it loosely")
- Cross-references name specific gaps ("Your positioning says design systems for scaling companies, but in three conversations you pitched general product design")
- Red flags presented as observations, not judgments

## Fail Indicators
- Every theme section populated regardless of signal strength
- Pattern language used for observations based on one or two conversations
- Existing knowledge files ignored when they exist
- Vague observations that could apply to anyone ("You sometimes hesitate on pricing")

## Weight
High — a padded or overconfident observation map poisons every downstream coaching conversation that builds on it.

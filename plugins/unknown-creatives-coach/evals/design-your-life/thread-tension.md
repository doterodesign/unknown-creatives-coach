# Eval: Cross-Thread Tension Surfacing

## Applies To
Design-your-life skill — any exchange where the user's statements across threads conflict (values vs. working style vs. life requirements)

## Criteria
- When the user's desires conflict across threads, the coach names the tension explicitly instead of letting it pass
- Tensions are framed as design decisions to make, not problems or contradictions to be embarrassed about
- Coach asks the user what they want to do about the tension rather than resolving it for them

## Pass Indicators
- Conflicts get named concretely ("You want to travel once a month, but you also said you prefer in-person client meetings. How do those coexist?")
- Framing is neutral and curious, not corrective
- The user is asked to choose or design around the tension ("Where's the line?")
- Surfaced tensions appear in the Life Design Summary as open design decisions

## Fail Indicators
- Obvious conflicts between stated desires pass without comment
- Coach resolves the tension unilaterally ("You'll just have to travel less")
- Tension framed as the user being inconsistent or unrealistic
- Tensions surfaced in conversation but dropped from the final output

## Weight
Medium — critical when tensions appear, but not every session surfaces one. Judges MUST check whether the user's statements actually conflicted before scoring. If no tension existed, score N/A and exclude from the weighted average.

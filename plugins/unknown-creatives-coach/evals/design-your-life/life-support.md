# Eval: Life the Business Supports Thread

## Applies To
Design-your-life skill — Thread 3: The Life the Business Needs to Support

## Criteria
- Coach helps the user articulate what their actual life should look like — not just the work part — and treats those desires as design requirements for the business
- Every personal desire gets translated into a business implication (financial floor, scheduling constraint, project filter)
- Coach pushes past vague goals to tangible specifics
- Desires are not judged — luxury and simplicity are equally valid design requirements

## Pass Indicators
- Coach asks whole-life questions ("If your business was working perfectly, what does a normal week look like? Not just the work hours.")
- Vague aspirations get concretized ("'Financial freedom' — what does that mean in numbers? What monthly lifestyle does the business need to cover?")
- Personal desires are explicitly translated ("Travel once a month → that shapes how projects are structured and how available you are")
- Big goals get named and sized (retiring parents, buying a home) as revenue targets

## Fail Indicators
- Thread reduced to work-life balance clichés without specifics
- Personal desires collected but never translated into business constraints
- Coach judges or moralizes about the user's desires (either luxury or modesty)
- "Financial freedom" or similar vagueness accepted as an answer

## Weight
High — this thread is the skill's distinctive contribution; its constraints feed services, pricing, and ICP directly.
